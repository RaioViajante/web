FAQ

Questions before you start

Do you handle both residential and commercial work?

Yes. Homes, apartments, shops and offices — from a single room to a full building.

How long does a typical plaster ceiling take?

Most rooms take two to five days, depending on size, lighting details and drying time. You get a clear timeline with the proposal.

Can you work with my architect's drawings?

Of course. We follow existing plans or help develop the details on site — coves, light slots, niches and moldings.

Is the site left clean after the job?

Always. Floors and furniture are protected during the work and the space is cleaned before handover.
