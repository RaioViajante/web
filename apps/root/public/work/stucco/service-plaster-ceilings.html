FAQ

About plaster ceilings

Will a lowered ceiling make my room feel smaller?

Usually only a few centimeters are needed. With coves and indirect light, rooms tend to feel taller, not lower.

Can I add lighting later?

It's best to plan it from the start so wiring and fixtures are positioned before the boards are closed.

Is plaster suitable for kitchens and bathrooms?

Yes, using moisture-resistant boards and the right paint system for wet areas.
