Hello, everyone.

I decided to build this website to post about my experiments, personal projects, things I'm learning, and whatever happens in between. Basically, a place to dump things from my brain before they disappear.

A little about me: I'm RaioViajante, an alter ego of someone else.

I'm just an enthusiast who really likes programming, hardware, and computers in general. Actually, "really likes" might be an understatement. This stuff makes me feel alive — even when it also makes me stare at an error message for three hours.

I don't want this place to be overly technical or "professional." I'm not here to pretend I know everything. Quite the opposite.

I want to learn in public, make mistakes, break things, fix some of them, and write about what happened.

Maybe, someday, this can become a small community too. I'd like to meet people who are into the same weird computer stuff I am. People I can share projects, ideas, discoveries, and questionable decisions with.

And, to be honest, I've been feeling pretty lonely lately. A little sad too.

Maybe this place can become somewhere I can actually be myself, without having to perform for anyone.

I don't really know what this will become yet.

That's kind of the point.

Worst case, I end up with a very overengineered diary.
